BEHIND THE COLLECTION

MORE THAN A
BASKETBALL SHOE.

From its debut in 1985, the Air Jordan evolved beyond performance footwear into a cultural symbol connected to sport, fashion, identity, and design. Born from the partnership between Michael Jordan and Nike, the original Air Jordan I introduced a new approach to basketball footwear, combining performance, distinctive design, and the influence of one of the sport's most recognizable rising stars.

As Michael Jordan's career progressed, so did the sneaker line. Each new generation introduced changes in materials, technology, construction, and visual identity, reflecting the evolution of both basketball and footwear design. What began as a signature basketball shoe gradually found a place far beyond the court, becoming closely associated with streetwear, music, fashion, and popular culture.

Over time, Air Jordan became more than a collection of athletic shoes. It developed into a form of personal expression, a symbol of cultural influence, and an important part of sneaker history. Its designs connected generations of athletes, collectors, and enthusiasts, demonstrating how a product created for sport could leave a lasting impression on the world of fashion and design.

This collection explores that evolution through 40 mainline models, tracing four decades of innovation, creativity, and cultural change. Each release represents another chapter in the story of a sneaker line that continually redefined what basketball footwear could become.
